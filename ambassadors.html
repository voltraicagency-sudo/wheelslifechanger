Ambassadors

Champions of Inclusion

Meet the inspiring individuals who represent the Wheels Life Changer mission — proving that disability is not inability.

Ambassador

Lesego Marema

At just four years old, Lesego inspires South Africa with her determination. She writes, draws, eats, and plays using only her feet — with extraordinary creativity and strength.

"In a world defined by limits, Lesego reminds us that resilience writes its own story."

Born with no arms, yet full of potential, she is not only a symbol of courage — she is a living light for others.

Ambassador

Nolitha Kibido

"From Xhorha to the World."

Nolitha's journey began in the small Eastern Cape village of Xhorha (Elliot Dale). Now a social work student at Stellenbosch University and an international wheelchair basketball athlete, she proves that disability is not inability.

Raised in Khayelitsha after losing both parents, she overcame adversity to compete globally and study what she lives — supporting others through social work.

"I'm here to give what I never had — hope, strength, and opportunity."
