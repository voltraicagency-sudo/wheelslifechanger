About Us

More Than an NGO —
A Movement of Possibility

Co-founded by a team of three visionaries, Wheels Life Changer is a registered NPO rooted in service, love, and action. Our focus is not on titles or names — but the lives we touch. We began with one mission: ensure that no child or adult is left behind due to disability.
